Delivery charges

Royal Mail International Tracked (under 1 kg / 1 kg or more):
• Europe except Germany: £10 / £25, 5–7 working days.
• Germany: £20 / £25, 5–7 working days.
• United States: £20 / £30, 7–10 working days. Contact us before ordering, as Royal Mail requires duties to be prepaid.
• Rest of World: £15 / £30, 7–10 working days.
The delivery charge and total payable must be confirmed before you place your order.

Dispatch and delivery times
We dispatch Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays. On working days, orders are processed and prepared within 24 hours. Please allow an extra working day over bank holidays.
Unless we agree otherwise, UK consumer orders must be delivered without undue delay and within 30 days of the contract being made. Contact kitaly.official@outlook.com if your delivery is late or missing.
