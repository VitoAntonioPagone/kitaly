Refunds
For a cancelled order, we refund payments including the least expensive standard outbound delivery option we offered. We do not have to refund the extra cost of an upgraded delivery service. We refund using the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise, without a refund fee.

Faulty or misdescribed goods
Vintage and secondhand goods remain covered by consumer law. Their age, price and accurately disclosed condition are relevant to what you can reasonably expect, but do not remove your rights. Where goods fail to conform to the contract, UK law normally provides a 30-day short-term right to reject, followed by repair or replacement rights and, in qualifying circumstances, a price reduction or final rejection. Contact us even if the change-of-mind period has ended.
